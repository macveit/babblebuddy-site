About

Independent software engineering

lunatic-enterprises is the independent software engineering initiative by Marcus Veit. With more than four decades in IT, cybersecurity and complex technical projects, the focus is shifting toward building small, useful and durable software products.

Selected consulting in architecture, project leadership and technical analysis is available on request, but the main focus is product development.
